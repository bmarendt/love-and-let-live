The Purpose

Why This Show Exists

This work began as a personal reckoning — the moment Blair told his wife, with total conviction, that she was going to hell, using love, in his own words, "as a weapon."

Love and Let Live carries that reckoning forward: to spend the rest of a life sharing love with people rather than judgment — and to show, through Jesus and through every other tradition that has arrived at the same place, that God is love, love is everywhere, and embracing it is how we meet our creator.
