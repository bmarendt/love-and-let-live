The Purpose

Why This Show Exists

This work began as a personal reckoning — the moment Blair told his wife, with total conviction, that she was going to hell, using love, in his own words, "as a weapon."

What came out of that was a question rather than an answer: what happens if God is love? Not love as one attribute among many — love as the whole of it. This show follows that premise as far as it goes: through Jesus, through every other tradition on the map, and straight into the passages that seem to refuse it.

That premise is a point on the map, not the destination. It is where the host is standing today, held loosely, open to revision the moment something truer turns up. Anyone is welcome to come along while the search continues.
